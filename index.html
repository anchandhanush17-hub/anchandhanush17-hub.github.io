My journey

I studied B.Sc. Computer Science at Jnan Vikas Mandal, Airoli, under the University of Mumbai. While studying, I found that I enjoy working with data more than anything else, so I decided to become a data analyst.

I started with an online Business Analyst course, then joined the ExcelR institute in Thane for a Data Analysis course. There I learned Excel, SQL, Power BI and Tableau, and worked on real datasets.

Next, I worked as a Data Analyst Intern at Ai Variant, where our team analysed bank debit and credit transactions. I kept practising on my own and built more dashboards. Now I am looking for my first role as a data analyst and I am also learning data science.
